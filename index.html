Frequently Asked Questions

Is Noshlog really free?

Yes. Barcode scanning, daily food logging, and weekly summaries are completely free. Forever. An optional Pro subscription ($2.99/month) removes ads and adds CSV data export.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Noshlog works without any account, email, or sign-up. Your data stays on your device. Just install and start scanning.

What food database does it use?

Noshlog uses Open Food Facts, the world's largest open food database with over 3 million products. You can also create custom foods with your own nutrition values.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Products you've scanned before are cached on your device. You can log meals without internet. New barcodes require a connection for the first lookup.

How is this different from MyFitnessPal?

Noshlog is simpler and more private. No account required, no social features, no server-side data storage. Just scan, log, and track. The core features are free forever, not behind a paywall.

What languages are supported?

English, Dutch, German, French, and Spanish. The app follows your device language automatically, or you can pick your preferred language in Settings.
